## Method

The method follows `gpu-prices-2026-11-06-rtx3090.md`. Where it differs, the table says so.

| | |
|---|---|
| Source | eBay US, sold listings, Sold Items + Completed Items, Used, US Only, Graphics/Video Cards. All 240 listings on the page are "Pre-Owned" and "Located in United States" |
| Search | `rtx 3090 -ti`. **Differs from 11-06:** that pull put most exclusions into the query (`-box -fan -waterblock -bracket …`). This one excludes by title, as 10-15 did, so complete-in-box and fan-mentioning listings are back in |
| Card filter | **By title.** Plain 3090 only: no Ti (none appeared), no lots, no accessories, no other cards |
| Window | **2026-11-13..11-23** (11 days), the run of days the page covers completely; three stragglers sold earlier. 11-06 used 13 days |
| Basis | **Sold price excluding shipping.** 147 of the 225 kept sales charged shipping; 3 show no delivery line and count as $0 in the shipping-included reference |
| Best offer | 19 of 225 are "Best offer accepted", kept at the struck-through **list** price, as on 11-06, with a without-them sensitivity |
| Discounted listings | Lines 1052 (`$1,799.99$2,700.00`) and 2582 (`$1,799.99$2,699.99`) show a sale price and a "was" price; the first (sold) price is used |
| Statistic | p25 / median / p75, `statistics.quantiles(method='inclusive')`, **rounded to $5** (half up), as on 11-06 |

The parser is the one used for the 2026-11-23 Ti and P40 record; it reproduces the 11-06 page's 240 sold blocks.

The floor held; the middle and top moved up. Part of the p75 move may be method: this query no longer drops
complete-in-box or fan-mentioning listings, which 11-06's record noted "may sell higher".

**Side check:** the 2026-11-23 3090 Ti page (`gpu-prices-2026-11-23-rtx3090ti-p40.md`) carried 193 plain-3090 sales in
11-15..11-23, filtered by title only, with a median of **$1,649**. This screened pull gives $1,649 unrounded. The two
pages are separate searches with overlapping sales, so this is agreement, not independent confirmation.

**3090 Ti premium:** against this band the Ti ($1,725 median, n=16) is about **$75** above a same-week 3090, down from
the ~$145 quoted against the 11-06 band.

## Caveats

- **Exclusion is by title**, as on 11-06. A mislabelled Ti or a mining card could be in the sample.
- **The raw file is eBay page chrome plus results.** Line 7 (the account greeting) and line 51 (the delivery ZIP) are
  replaced with `[redacted page chrome]`. Line numbers are unchanged. The file does not match the mirror's `PII_GATE`.
- **Best offer:** list price, not accepted price, as before. Without those sales the median is $1,625.
