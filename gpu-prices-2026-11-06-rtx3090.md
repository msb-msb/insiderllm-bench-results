## Against the 2026-10-15 band ($1,397 / $1,499 / $1,552, n=161, 7 days)

| | 10-15 | 11-06 (unrounded) | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| p25 | $1,397 | $1,499.99 | +$103 (+7.4%) |
| median | $1,499 | $1,582.00 | +$83 (+5.5%) |
| p75 | $1,552 | $1,623.00 | +$71 (+4.6%) |

**Noise check.**
- **Method:** a bootstrap of this sample, 20,000 resamples. The 10-15 raw listings aren't in the repo, so the 10-15 quartiles' sampling error is estimated from this distribution at n=161.
- **Results:** the 95% interval is $1,550-1,599 for the median and $1,475-1,500 for the p25. The approximate z for the change is 7.8 at the p25, 3.5 at the median and 2.2 at the p75.
- **Conclusion:** the p25 and median moves are well outside sampling noise. The p75 move is borderline on its own. The weekly medians ($1,522, then $1,599) point the same way.

**Not purely market movement.** Three method differences are present, and their sizes aren't measured:
- The query excludes `-box` and `-fan`. These drop any listing whose title says "with box" or mentions a fan, so complete-in-box cards, which may sell higher, are missing. The p25 doesn't depend on them.
- The window is 13 days, not 7.
- The quartile rule used on 10-15 isn't recorded.

None of these looks large enough to explain an $83 median move. The prices sit on round numbers ($1,500, $1,599, $1,600), so the quartiles move in steps.

## Caveats

- **Exclusion is by title**, as on 10-15. A mislabelled Ti or a mining card could be in the sample.
- **The raw file is eBay page chrome plus results.** Lines 7 (the account greeting) and 50 (the delivery ZIP) are replaced with `[redacted page chrome]`. Line numbers are unchanged, so the references above still hold. Line 56, "Update your location25 miles", is eBay's search radius and holds no ZIP.
